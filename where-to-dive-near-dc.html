Where to dive near DC

Places DC-area divers actually go, from a weekend quarry trip to a wreck-diving road trip. Each guide is checked against the site's own pages and dated.

Open all year About 3 hours

Lake Phoenix, Virginia

Former rock quarry near Petersburg, about 60 ft deep, with sunk buses, boats, a plane and a helicopter. Where many DC divers do their Open Water dives. Camping on site.

 Weekends About 2¼ hours

Lake Allure, Pennsylvania

Small, quiet private quarry in Lancaster County with training platforms and room for only 30 divers. Buy tickets online. No solo diving.

 Weekends About 3 hours

Willow Springs, Pennsylvania

Spring-fed training quarry, about 55 ft deep, with a sunk Learjet, a 72-ft trawler and a school bus. Air fills on site. Same pass as Lake Allure.

 Supervised only About 3¾ hours

Lake Hydra, Pennsylvania

The former Dutch Springs, reopened in 2024 for training. About 100 ft deep. Every diver must be a dive professional or dive with one.

 Summer only About 5 hours

Summersville Lake, West Virginia

Clear water and sandstone cliffs that drop about 100 ft. Dive from the boat with Sarge's, the floating dive shop. Memorial Day to Labor Day.

 Road trip About 6 hours

North Carolina wrecks

World War II wrecks and sand tiger sharks off Morehead City and Beaufort. Shallower wrecks for newer divers, deep ones for experienced divers.

Closed, private or not worth the drive

Older lists and dive shop pages still mention these. Here's what we found.

  • Hyde's Quarry, near Westminster, Maryland: closed to divers after the 2018 season, when zebra mussels were found and the county ended the diving lease.
  • Millbrook Quarry, Haymarket, Virginia: closed since 2020.
  • Guppy Gulch, Delta, Pennsylvania: now runs as a private camp and water park for organized groups and says it isn't open to the public. We couldn't confirm that scuba diving is still offered. Ask your dive shop before you go.
  • Mount Storm Lake, West Virginia: warm all year because it cools a power plant, but the owner, Dominion Energy, now allows diving only from a boat, not from shore.
  • Lake Rawlings, Virginia: not a separate place. It's Lake Phoenix's old name.

Know something we don't, like a quarry that has reopened? Use the tip box on any site page or email hello@divefolk.com.

How far from DC?

Driving miles and rough times from the White House, checked on Google Maps on November 27, 2026. Traffic, especially on I-95, can add a lot.

  • Lake Allure, Quarryville, PA: about 110 miles, 2¼ hours
  • Lake Phoenix, Rawlings, VA: about 165 miles, 3 hours
  • Willow Springs, Richland, PA: about 165 miles, 3 hours
  • Lake Hydra, Bethlehem, PA: about 205 miles, 3¾ hours
  • Summersville Lake, Summersville, WV: about 315 miles, 5 hours
  • North Carolina wrecks, Morehead City, NC: about 370 miles, 6 hours

Before any local dive

  • Check the site's own calendar. Quarry hours change by season, and some days need a reservation.
  • Plan your wetsuit for the cold water below the thermocline, not the warm surface.
  • Fill in our free shore dive emergency plan for the site.
  • Not sure your gear and skills are ready? A shop group day is the easiest way to start.

Checked November 27, 2026. Sources: each site's page; ScubaBoard reports on Hyde's Quarry closing; DiveBuddy on Millbrook Quarry; Guppy Gulch; Dominion Energy on Mt. Storm Lake; Scuba Diving magazine on Lake Rawlings. Drive times from Google Maps.
