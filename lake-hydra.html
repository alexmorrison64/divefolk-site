Where to dive near DC · Pennsylvania quarry

Lake Hydra, Pennsylvania

The famous Dutch Springs quarry in Bethlehem, PA closed in 2021 and reopened in 2024 as Lake Hydra. The big change: it's now a training site, and you can't just show up and fun dive.

The one thing to know first

Everyone in the water must be an insured, certified dive professional or be supervised by one. Lake Hydra is open to dive schools, organizations, and insured instructors and divemasters with their students. If you're a certified diver who just wants to dive, book through a dive shop or a guide service that runs trips there, then buy your entry ticket with the code they give you.

At a glance

  • Where: 4733 Hanoverville Road, Bethlehem, PA 18020. Roughly 200 miles from DC, so plan on a long day or an overnight.
  • Season: April through November. Opens at 8 AM.
  • Tickets: online only, at least 24 hours ahead. No sales at the gate. Instructors with students and divemasters helping a class get in free.
  • Depth: about 100 feet at the deepest.
  • Air and nitrox: air and 32% nitrox fills on site.
  • Buddies: required for everyone, including instructors. No solo diving.
  • Contact: info@lakehydra.com, lakehydra.com

What's underwater

Many of Dutch Springs' sunk attractions are still there, including a Sikorsky helicopter, a Cessna airplane and a boat called The Minnow, plus an old pump house. Look for koi and other freshwater fish.

How to book a fun dive

  • Ask your own dive shop if it runs Lake Hydra trips.
  • Or use a guide service near the lake. Several shops in the Bethlehem area sell guided dives and give you a code to buy your entry ticket.
  • Guided dives cost extra on top of the entry ticket; check what's included (tanks, weights, rentals).
