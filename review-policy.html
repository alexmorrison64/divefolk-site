Review policy

How we keep reviews honest

Short version: we tell you who paid, we keep the bad news in, and no business gets to approve a review before you read it.

Who this covers

Everyone who writes for DiveFolk, including our founder. That means trip reports, gear reviews, reviews of dive shops, resorts and dive sites, and guest articles.

We tell you who paid

Every review says how the writer got the trip, gear or service. You'll see a line near the top like one of these:

  • Paid in full. The writer paid the normal price.
  • Discounted. The writer got a lower price than other divers would pay.
  • Free or hosted. The business gave the gear, dives, room or trip at no cost.
  • Loaned. The gear was lent for testing and given back.

We also tell you about any other connection that might matter to you, such as the writer having trained with the shop, working for it, or being friends with the owners.

What never happens

  • No business sees, edits or approves a review before it's published.
  • Nobody is paid to say something good. Free gear or a hosted trip never comes with a promise of a good review, or of any review at all.
  • We don't remove honest criticism in exchange for anything.
  • Writers only review trips they took, sites they dove and gear they used.
  • Owners and staff don't review their own business, or a competitor's.
  • We never make up reviews or write them in someone else's name.

Free gear

If a company sends gear to review, the review says so and says what happened to it afterward: kept, sent back, or given away.

If a business thinks we got something wrong

We fix factual mistakes, like a wrong price, date or boat schedule, and note the date of the correction. A business can also send a reply, and we may add it below the review. We won't change a writer's opinion because a business disagrees with it.

Sponsors, ads and affiliate links

DiveFolk has no sponsors, ads or affiliate links today. If that ever changes, we'll update this page and our privacy policy first, and anything sponsored will be clearly labeled "Sponsored." Sponsors will never have a say over reviews.

News

In DiveFolk News, every story is checked against its original source. Press releases, giveaways and tourism promotions are labeled "Promotional."

People in our stories

We don't name or show dive buddies, staff or other divers without their OK, and we never name private people who were hurt in an accident.

Writing for DiveFolk

If you send us a trip report, review or article, you agree to follow this policy. Tell us how you paid and any connection you have to the business. We read everything before it goes up, and we may trim it for length or clarity, but we won't change your verdict without asking you. See DiveFolk Trips for what to include in a trip report.

A note on safety

Reviews describe one diver's experience on particular days. Conditions change, and a review is not professional advice. Always dive within your training and follow your dive professional's briefing.
