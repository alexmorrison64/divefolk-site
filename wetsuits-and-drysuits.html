Technology Explained · Staying warm

Wetsuit or drysuit for cold quarries?

Local quarries can be warm at the top and cold below the thermocline. Here's how to choose, and how to get a fit that works, including if you're bigger or taller than the size chart expects.

Dress for the coldest water

Choose your suit for the coldest water you'll reach, not the surface. At Lake Phoenix, for example, the park says the summer thermocline is around 44 to 58°F, even when the surface is over 80°F.

  • 3mm wetsuit: warm water, or shallow summer quarry dives above the thermocline.
  • 5mm to 7mm wetsuit, with hood and gloves: most quarry diving in spring, summer and fall if you go deep.
  • Drysuit: water in the 50s and below, long dives, or if you get cold easily.

Everyone runs differently. If you shiver, end the dive; cold makes you breathe harder and think slower.

How a wetsuit should fit

  • Snug everywhere, like a second skin, with no gaps at the lower back, armpits or knees. Loose spots let cold water flush through.
  • You can lift your arms over your head and squat without fighting the suit.
  • The neck seals without choking you.
  • Try it on in the shop, and move around in it.

Drysuits

  • A drysuit keeps you dry; the undergarment underneath keeps you warm. Try it on with the undergarment you'll wear.
  • It should let you reach your tank valve and your fins, and kneel, without pulling.
  • Take a drysuit course before you dive one. It handles differently, and many shops ask for drysuit training before they'll rent you one.
  • Don't buy cheap here. A drysuit that leaks or fits badly is miserable and less safe.

For bigger or taller divers

  • Look for extended size lines. Many makers sell "tall," "short" and "plus" or "big" versions of their regular sizes.
  • Check more than one brand's size chart. Cuts vary a lot; some brands run slimmer, others roomier.
  • For drysuits, ask about made-to-measure. Several major makers offer it, using around 20 body measurements instead of 3 or 4. It costs more and takes extra weeks, so order well before your trip.
  • If you order made-to-measure through a shop, it's fair to confirm with the maker that you got a custom cut.
