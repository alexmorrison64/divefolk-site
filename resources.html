DiveFolk Commons · Other Resources

The best of the diving web

Communities, safety, conservation, news and tools that we think are worth your time. DiveFolk isn't affiliated with any of them, and nothing here is sponsored.

Forums and groups

ScubaBoard

A large, long-running scuba forum covering trip planning, gear, training and dive sites.

r/scuba on Reddit

Questions and discussion for divers of every level, from new divers to instructors.

r/UnderwaterPhotography on Reddit

Cameras, housings, lighting and technique for underwater photos.

DeeperBlue forums

Forums for freediving and scuba, alongside DeeperBlue's news.

Find a dive buddy or club

DiveBuddy

A free online dive community with forums, buddy matching and dive site details.

Your local dive shop

Most shops run fun dives, trips and social nights. It's the easiest way to meet divers near you.

Dive safety and health

DAN (Divers Alert Network)

Dive safety research, medical information for divers, an emergency hotline, and dive insurance.

Citizen science and conservation

REEF

Learn fish identification and report what you see through REEF's volunteer fish surveys.

Project AWARE

Ocean conservation, including Dive Against Debris surveys of underwater trash.

iNaturalist

Share photos of marine life to get help with identification and add to biodiversity records.

Magazines and news

Scuba Diving magazine

Travel, gear reviews, training and an annual dive travel calendar.

Undercurrent

An independent, reader-supported newsletter with dive travel reviews and consumer reporting.

The Scuba News

Daily diving news, features and industry updates.

Divernet

Diving news and features from the UK.

DeeperBlue

News and features on freediving, scuba and ocean sports.

Tools

Subsurface

Free, open-source dive log software for computers and phones that works with many dive computers.

Windy

Wind, wave and weather forecasts, useful when planning shore and boat dives.
