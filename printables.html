Shore dive emergency plan

Fill it in for your own site before your dive day: the address to give 911, the nearest hospital, where the oxygen, first aid kit and AED are, who in your group is trained, and the steps to follow. It includes the DAN Emergency Hotline, +1-919-684-9111; call 911 first, then DAN.

Download the emergency plan (PDF)

These aren't a substitute for training. They're memory aids. Follow your instructor, your training agency, your dive operator and your dive computer, and take a Rescue Diver, first aid or oxygen course if you haven't.
