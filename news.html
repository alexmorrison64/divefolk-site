How DiveFolk News works

  • We scan diving news sources for stories divers care about.
  • A person reviews every story before it's posted. Nothing is published automatically.
  • Summaries are short and in our own words, and they always link to the original.
  • Press releases and giveaways are labeled as such.
  • New stories are added once a week.
