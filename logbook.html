DiveFolk Logbook Beta

Clean up your dive log

Add the files from your dive computer, check how DiveFolk grouped them, then download your log. Nothing is uploaded; it all happens in this browser window.

1. Add your dives

In Shearwater Cloud: select your dives, then File, Export, As UDDF.

2. Check your dives

What do these columns mean?
Same dive
DiveFolk joins pieces your computer split apart, such as a quick trip to the surface or a false start. Untick it to keep them as separate dives.
Use piece
Untick to leave one piece out, for example a training exercise before the real dive.
Log it
Untick to skip a dive, such as a pool session.
Clock fix
If your computer was on the wrong time zone, move the dive by whole hours.
Log itDive #Same diveUse pieceClock fixDateStart MinutesMax ftMin °F Start psiEnd psiComputer #

3. Download your log

The CSV opens in any spreadsheet. DL7 is the dive log format from DAN (Divers Alert Network); some dive log programs can import it. Each dive includes depth, water temperature and tank pressure readings.
