Clean up your dive log

Add the files from your dive computer, check how DiveFolk grouped them, then download your log. Works with Shearwater, Garmin and other computers that export UDDF. Nothing is uploaded; it all happens in this browser window.

1. Add your dives

Not sure where your files are? See the steps below.

How to get your dive files

Shearwater

In Shearwater Cloud on a computer, select your dives, then choose File, Export, As UDDF. Drop the files here.

Garmin, one dive at a time

Sign in at connect.garmin.com on a computer (the phone app can't export). Open Activities, open a dive, click the gear icon at the top right, then choose Export Original. Drop the .zip you get here, as it is. You can drop several at once.

Garmin, every dive at once

In Garmin Connect, go to your account settings and find Export Your Data (it may be under Data Management or Privacy). Garmin emails you a download link, usually within a couple of days. Drop that whole .zip here. DiveFolk finds the dives and skips your runs, walks and other activities.

If the file is very large (over about 4 GB) and won't open, unzip it and drop the files named UploadedFiles...zip instead.

Other computers

Many dive apps and Subsurface can export UDDF. Look for Export, then UDDF. More computers are coming; tell us which one you use.

Does your computer split one dive into two?

DiveFolk joins them for you below. To stop it happening, see our guide to split dives for every major brand.

2. Check your dives

What do these columns mean?
Same dive
DiveFolk joins pieces your computer split apart, such as a quick trip to the surface or a false start. Untick it to keep them as separate dives.
Use piece
Untick to leave one piece out, for example a training exercise before the real dive.
Log it
Untick to skip a dive, such as a pool session.
Clock fix
If your computer was on the wrong time zone, move the dive by whole hours.

3. Download your log

The CSV opens in any spreadsheet. DL7 is the dive log format from DAN (Divers Alert Network); some dive log programs can import it. Each dive includes depth and water temperature readings, plus tank pressure when your computer records it.

Optional: add your paper logbook

Your paper logbook has things your computer doesn't: the site name, your buddy, visibility, weights and what you saw. DiveFolk can put those notes on the right computer dives for you.

  1. Take a clear photo of each logbook page.
  2. Copy the prompt below and give it to your own AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another) along with the photos. Your photos go to your assistant, not to DiveFolk.
  3. Copy the table it gives you and paste it into the box below, or choose the CSV file it made.
Show the prompt 
I'm attaching photos of my paper dive logbook. For each logged dive, make one row. Start with a header row using exactly these column names, in this order:
Dive #, Date, Time in, Time out, Bottom time (min), Max depth (ft), Dive site, Location, Country, Buddy, Water temp at depth (°F), Visibility (ft), Start pressure (psi), End pressure (psi), Weight (lb), Exposure suit, Notes

Write dates as YYYY-MM-DD and times like 10:05 AM. Give me the result as one table I can copy, or as a CSV file I can download. If something is hard to read, leave that cell blank and list it under "Please check" with the dive number. Don't guess.

No computer files? You can still paste your paper log and download it as a clean spreadsheet.
